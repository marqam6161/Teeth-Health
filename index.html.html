What we treat

Four common treatments, done at the clinic by Dr Noman.

  • Filling

    A cavity is cleaned out and the tooth is filled, so decay stops and you can chew normally again.

  • Cleaning

    Plaque and tartar are removed from your teeth and along the gum line, which helps prevent gum disease and bad breath.

  • Artificial teeth

    Missing teeth are replaced with artificial ones shaped and shaded to match the teeth around them.

  • Root canal

    When infection reaches the inside of a tooth, the pulp is cleaned out and sealed. This relieves the pain and saves the tooth from being pulled.
